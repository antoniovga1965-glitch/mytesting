Welcome to Smart Bursary Distribution Portal

Apply for financial support — transparent, fair, and powered by AI verification.

👥 Who is Eligible?

  • Students with no parents (orphans)
  • Students with single parents
  • Students who are disabled
  • Students whose parents live beyond the poverty line

📋 How to Apply

  1. Fill in the registration form with correct credentials
  2. Upload necessary documents in each section
  3. After completing, click the Submit button
  4. Wait for processing and verification

📁 Required Materials

Ensure you have the following before applying:

  1. KCPE results slip
  2. Recent results slip
  3. Any local authority document (Chief's letter)
  4. ID copy / Birth certificate
  5. Admission letter
  6. Death certificate of any deceased parent
⚠️

Warning: Any upload of fake documents will lead to permanent elimination from the bursary process and may result in legal action.
